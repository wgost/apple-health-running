Performance

This section uses outdoor runs only, so treadmill distance does not blur the comparisons. Read the metrics as trends, not scores: most have no universal target, so compare runs at a similar pace and temperature.

  • Efficiency: metres per heartbeat; higher is better. Look for a rise at the same pace.
  • Power: no universal target (it scales with body weight). The goal is the same or lower HR at the same power.
  • Stride length: no target (it grows with speed and height). The goal is more of it at the same effort, with cadence around 165–180 steps/min.
  • Cardiac drift: under 5% on an easy run is good, 5–10% is common, and above 10% points to heat, fatigue or starting too fast.
  • Temperature: context only. Heat above roughly 70°F21°C raises HR and lowers efficiency, so a dip on a hot day is not lost fitness.
