Running report

Turn your Apple Health export into a running report. Your file is read in this browser tab and is never uploaded.

How do I get export.zip?
  1. On your iPhone open the Health app and tap your picture (top right).
  2. Scroll down and tap Export All Health Data, then wait for it to finish.
  3. Save the zip to Files (or AirDrop it to your computer), then choose it above.

The file can be several GB; reading it takes a minute or two. Keep this tab open and in the foreground.
